Part 3 · Peak District · Peak District · Day 16 of 20

Fri 25 Dec

Christmas Day hike

Plan and checklist

  • 07:15–07:45
    Early breakfast and pack for the hike
    Warm layers, hats, gloves, waterproofs, boots, flask, snacks and a phone torch. Sunrise is about 08:20 and sunset about 15:52: be off the hill well before dark
    Christmas Day: shops and cafés are shut, so bring all food and drinks (buy them on 24 Dec)
  • 07:45–08:25
    Drive to Mam Nick Car Park
    About 35–40 min via Hathersage and the Hope Valley. Satnav: Mam Nick Car Park (also called Mam Tor Hillfort Car Park), S33 8WA · Mam Nick Car Park on Google Maps
    National Trust car park: £6 for 4 hours, £8 all day, free for NT members. Pay by the JustPark app only (no cash or card machine). Set the app up at home, because phone signal can be poor there. No toilets
    Very small car park that fills early. Back-ups: the 55 free bays at the top of Old Mam Tor Road (about 50 min walk up), or Castleton Car Park (£4.50 for 2–4 h, toilets) · Castleton Car Park on Google Maps
    Do not park on the verges: Old Mam Tor Road and nearby lanes have been red routes since May 2026 (£70 fine)
    £6 parking
  • 08:30–09:15
    Climb Mam Tor (517 m)
    A paved path and stone steps from the car park: about 15 min up, 25–35 min with photo stops. Short but steep, so go at your own pace
    Big views over the Hope Valley, Edale and Kinder Scout. It is windy and much colder on top
    Free
  • 09:15–10:45
    Great Ridge walk to Hollins Cross
    Follow the paved ridge path east to Hollins Cross (the low saddle with views down to Edale and Castleton), then return the same way
    Mam Tor and Hollins Cross out and back takes about 2–3 hours in total. Fit and keen? Carry on to Back Tor and Lose Hill (about 7 miles as a circular walk from Castleton)
    Plan B in cloud or strong wind: Baslow Edge and Curbar Edge, 10–15 min from the cottage
    Free
  • 11:00–11:45
    Drive back through Winnats Pass and Hope Valley
    From Mam Nick the road drops through Winnats Pass, a dramatic limestone gorge. Drive through and do not stop on the road
    About 35–40 min back to Baslow. Change into dry clothes before lunch
  • 13:30–15:30
    Christmas lunch (pre-booked)
    The Wheatsheaf, Baslow: 3-course Christmas Day set menu. Or The Devonshire Arms: 3 courses £110 per adult, £55 for children
    Christmas Day tables are by reservation only, so book as soon as you can
    £110 pp (Devonshire Arms)
  • 16:00 onwards
    Evening at the cottage
    Light supper at home (see the Food plan). Sunset is about 15:52, so enjoy the dark evening indoors
    Check tomorrow's weather for the Ladybower walk before bed

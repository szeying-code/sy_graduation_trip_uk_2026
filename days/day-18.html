Good to know about Salzburg

Other attractions

Hellbrunn Palace (check winter opening), Mozart Residence, and the Untersberg cable car if it is running and the sky is clear.

Christmas market tip

The Mirabellplatz market runs 19 Nov – 31 Dec 2026, a convenient option on either evening.

Our recommendation

Don't squeeze in Hellbrunn and Untersberg too. The fortress, Old Town and Christmas market are enough for a very good visit.

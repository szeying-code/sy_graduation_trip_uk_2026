Part 3 · Peak District · Mansfield · Day 18 of 20

Sun 27 Dec

Peak District → Mansfield

Plan and checklist

  • 08:00–08:40
    Breakfast, pack the car, check out (by 10:00)
    Check-out is 10:00. Pack, tidy and hand back the key before you go for the walk, so there is no rush later. Take all food leftovers
    Sunrise is about 08:21 and sunset about 15:53
  • 08:40–09:00
    Drive to Curbar Gap Car Park
    About 10 min. Satnav: Curbar Gap Car Park, Clodhall Lane, S32 3YR · Curbar Gap Car Park on Google Maps
    National Trust car park: free for NT members, otherwise about £4 for 4 hours or £6 all day (listing; cash or card). No toilets. A snack van (Jolly's Coffee and Cake) is often there at weekends and school holidays
    ~£4–6 parking
  • 09:00–10:45
    Curbar Edge and Baslow Edge walk
    Start on Curbar Edge for wide views across the Derwent Valley, then follow the flat path along Baslow Edge to Wellington's Monument, with a view south towards Chatsworth
    The Baslow Edge circuit is 1.7 miles (2.7 km), about 1–1.5 hours. Add Curbar Edge for a longer loop. Look out for the Highland cows and the Eagle Stone
    Free
  • 12:00–13:00
    Sunday lunch in Baslow
    The Devonshire Arms (Sunday 12:00–22:00, Sunday roast) or The Wheatsheaf, Baslow (food 12:00–21:00). Book a table for 5 if you can. See the Food plan
    Lunch
  • 13:15–14:00
    Drive to Mansfield and check in at Pine Lodge
    About 22 miles, roughly 35 min. Pine Lodge Hotel & Restaurant, 281 Nottingham Road, Mansfield NG18 4SE · Pine Lodge on Google Maps
    Check-in 14:00–22:00. Tell the hotel your arrival time, because reception hours are short
  • 14:30–17:30
    Family day
    Rest after the drive, then family time. Tomorrow is packing day and Wei's last day
  • 18:30
    Dinner at Pine Lodge Restaurant
    Turkish and Mediterranean hotel restaurant, listed open Sunday 13:00–22:00. See the Food plan

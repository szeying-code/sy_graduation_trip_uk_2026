Part 3 · Peak District · Peak District · Day 17 of 20

Sat 26 Dec

Ladybower

Plan and checklist

  • 07:45–08:20
    Breakfast, pack and leave Baslow
    Boxing Day is busy: aim to be parked by about 09:15. Sunrise is about 08:21 and sunset about 15:53
    Take water, snacks and layers. Toilets are only at Fairholmes and Heatherdene
  • 08:20–09:00
    Drive to Fairholmes Car Park
    About 35–40 min. Satnav: Fairholmes Car Park, Upper Derwent Visitor Centre, S33 0AQ · Fairholmes Car Park on Google Maps
    Pay & display, cash or card: £3.50 up to 2 h, £6 for 2–4 h, £7 over 4 h. Or pay with RingGo zone 58251 (Severn Trent prices, listed 2025)
    If it is full: Derwent Overlook Car Park, 300 m back along the access road (RingGo 51707), or free roadside bays on Upper Derwent Valley Road
    £6–7 parking
  • 09:00–09:30
    Derwent Dam and the visitor centre
    Walk to the twin-towered Derwent Dam, a training site for the Dambusters. Easy tarmac, about 1–2 miles return
    Toilets, visitor centre and a small takeaway café at Fairholmes
    Free
  • 09:30–12:00
    Ladybower Reservoir loop
    From Fairholmes follow the reservoir path to Ashopton Viaduct, cross it, then return along the other bank. About 6 miles (9–10 km), easy and mostly flat, 2–2.5 hours
    Shorter option: Derwent Dam and back, 1–2 miles, about 45 min
    Mountains and reservoir views (Win Hill, Crook Hill). Paths can be muddy: wear boots
    Free
  • 12:30–13:30
    Lunch near Ladybower
    The Ladybower Inn (A57, open Thu–Sun) or The George, Hathersage (open daily). Phone to check Boxing Day hours. See the Food plan
    Back-up: a picnic from the cottage
    Lunch
  • 13:45–14:45
    Ladybower Dam and the 'plughole' overflows
    Heatherdene Car Park, S33 0BY: pay & display (bring coins to be safe), toilets and picnic benches · Heatherdene Car Park on Google Maps
    A short, easy walk to see Ladybower Dam and the giant overflow "plugholes". Free roadside bays near Heatherdene if the car park is full
    ~£3.50–6 parking
  • 15:00–16:00
    Drive back to Baslow before dark
    About 35–40 min. Sunset is about 15:53, so leave on time and take the country roads slowly
    Dinner: The Devonshire Arms or The Wheatsheaf, Baslow. See the Food plan

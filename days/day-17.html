Good to know about Hallstatt

Salt mine and funicular times

Salzwelten lists the mine 09:30–15:00 and the funicular 09:00–16:30 for 2 Nov 2026 – 6 Jan 2027. The last guided tour starts before closing, so check the tour slot and book ahead.

Obertraun?

Possible if weather and transport work, but not the main plan. The Krippenstein winter season starts on 19 Dec 2026, after your stay.

Our recommendation

Prioritise the Skywalk, salt mine and village over travelling between villages. Gosausee is a beautiful mountain lake, only if winter transport and weather allow.

Good to know about Prague

Extra options if you have energy

Vltava River cruise for city views from the water. Letná Park viewpoint for panoramic views of the bridges. Café Louvre for coffee and cake.

Christmas market

The Old Town Square and Wenceslas Square markets are listed for 28 Nov 2026 – 6 Jan 2027, so both should be running.

Winter timing

Prague Castle's historic buildings have winter hours of 09:00–16:00, so go in the morning.

Final tipsOur advice for December

Don't start too late

Aim to leave the hotel around 08:30–09:00 on full sightseeing days.

Use daylight for views

Put castles, viewpoints, lakes and outdoor walks earlier in the day.

Evenings for Christmas markets

These cities look especially beautiful when illuminated.

Waterproof shoes

Old-town streets can be slippery, and Hallstatt may have snow or ice.

Don't force every attraction

Leave time for Mum and Dad to enjoy the scenery, take pictures and sit down for coffee.
